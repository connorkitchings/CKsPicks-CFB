- **Attribution arms** (population, outcomes, design, alpha-10 recipe and the 2020 exclusion fixed in every arm): baseline = legacy Silver, baseline ledger, baseline offsets (served r9 / r9cert); EPA-only = corrected Silver with the baseline ledger and baseline offsets; scoring-only = legacy Silver with the admitted ledger in measurement and ratings but baseline offsets; offset-only = baseline ratings with offsets recomputed from the admitted ledger; combined = all corrections (the published outputs). Scoring reaches the forecast through two channels (PPP numerators and non-offense points), which is why scoring-only and offset-only are separate. The EPA-only arm must reproduce the baseline rating record hashes, offsets, feature frame, bundle and forecasts exactly (the selected design uses PPP only); any difference stops the run. The report includes the interaction term (combined minus baseline minus the sum of the single-factor effects), raw and adjusted materiality separately, and residual baseline errors separately from deltas. Forecasts are historical in-sample predictions of each arm's own bridge on its own feature frame; no 2026 forecasts (6B scope).
- **Published comparison (read-only, Preview only).** Preview tables `team_season_stats`, `team_possession_stats`, `team_rating_components`, `v5_rating_snapshots` and `matchup_data_publications` (verified readable by `cks_preview_pipeline`; 2026, as_of weeks 0-5) in a read-only transaction through `scripts/ops/with_preview_env.sh`, plus hash-pinned research artifacts in R2. No production database access and no read of production prefixes. Every difference must fall in a named bucket (population, punt-return plays, missing PPA, scoring); an unattributed difference is reported as a finding and no population is redefined to obtain agreement. Seasons before 2026 have no website publication and are compared only with the served r9 and r9cert artifacts. Preview equals production only as of the user-run publish; no stronger claim is made.
- **Receipt.** A signed (checksum signing, not cryptographic authentication) `rebuild_6a_receipt_v1` carrying identity, every input pin, exact output refs (dataset versions, artifact keys, hashes, catalog state), coverage, validation evidence, the attribution and published-comparison results, residuals and limits, the exact inputs for 6B, and explicit non-claims (no production activation, not all source defects repaired, Preview-only, user-specified CFBD rights basis, no serving, selection or authorization writes). It is published create-once under `rebuild/6a/6a-task4-r1/receipt/` after the user's confirmation, with a documentation summary carrying its hash; a final stage re-verifies every claim from the published objects.
