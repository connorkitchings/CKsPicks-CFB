## 1. Executive Summary & Context

The goal of this evaluation is to establish a permanent, authoritative brand identity for the college football platform, permanently retiring the working title **"CK's Picks"**.

### Why "CK's Picks" Must Be Retired
While "CK's Picks" served as a convenient working title during early model development, the word **"Picks"** is actively counterproductive in advanced sports analytics:
- It connotes commercial handicapping "touts," subscription tipster services, or casual betting pick'ems.
- It obscures the engineered rigor of the platform.

### What the Platform Actually Represents
The platform is an independent, quantitative college football rating and forecasting system built on:
- **V5 Blitzkrieg Engine:** Possession-based ratings, Ridge regression bridge, and drive-level efficiency.
- **Strict Methodological Integrity:** Opponent adjustment performed once at the measurement layer, strict temporal separation (point-in-time loading; no target leakage), and transparent performance tracking against closing market spreads and totals.
- **Dual Perspective (Accomplishment vs. Expectation):**
  - **Demonstrated Accomplishment (Résumé):** What did a team achieve, against what schedule, and under what conditions? (SOR, SOS, net EPA).
  - **Underlying Predictive Strength (Ratings):** What does the totality of evidence tell us about a team's true capability on a neutral field?
- **Interactive Visual Data Science:** Net success distributions, matchup unit tables, scatter plots, and rating progression trajectories.

The brand must command immediate respect among college football analysts, data scientists, and serious fans without sounding like a gambling tout or a generic enterprise software product.

## 2. Guiding Criteria & Evaluation Principles

Throughout iterative exploration and testing, the following non-negotiable boundaries were established:

1. **No Algorithmic Lock-In:**
   - Names tied to specific mathematical transformations (e.g., *Ridge Rank*) or singular data units (*Possession CFB*) were rejected. Statistical architectures evolve over time; the brand must outlive any single engine iteration.
2. **No Generic Military Tropes:**
   - Names like *Blitz CFB* or *Crucible* were rejected. "Blitz" is an overused sports media cliché; aggressive militaristic framing distracts from calm, empirical data.
3. **No HR / Job-Application Confusion:**
   - While the concept of a team's "résumé" (accomplishment given schedule) is foundational to the platform's analysis, the literal word *Résumé* was immediately discarded due to confusion with employment CVs.
4. **No Promotional Hype or False Finality:**
   - Superlative marketing terms (*Apex*, *Pinnacle*, *Optimum*, *Maximum*) read as boastful or promotional rather than analytical.
5. **No Negative Limit or Stagnation Associations:**
   - Words indicating constraints or stopping points (*Ceiling*, *Cap*, *Plateau*, *Threshold*) or transient noise (*Spike*) were disqualified.
6. **The Sweet Spot:**
   - A distinct, evocative noun combining **physical substance**, **relative competitive standing** (tiers/strata), and **collegiate or gridiron resonance**.

## 4. Creative Screening & Category Eliminations

To test the boundaries of team trajectory, peak performance, and analytical geometry, a comprehensive exploration was conducted. The table below documents the rationale for each candidate:

| Decision | Candidate Names | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| **Keep (Final 3)** | **Stratum**, **Crest**, **Echelon** | The three strongest contenders balancing analytical philosophy, physical substance, spoken clarity, and clean competitive runway. |
| **Final Look (Challenger)** | **Vertex** | Sharp, recognizable, visually striking. Bridges geometry and analytical thinking, but leans closer to enterprise technical software than an independent college football outlet. |
| **Eliminate: Too generic or promotional** | *Peak, Maximum, Optimum, Apex, Zenith, Summit, Acme, Pinnacle* | Mostly communicate "the highest" or "the best." They convey less perspective on relative competition than *Stratum* or *Echelon*. *Summit* had initial appeal, but existing sports analytics overlap weakened it. |
| **Eliminate: Narrower or less appealing meaning** | *Mode, Extremum, Inflection, Spike, Ridge* | *Mode* suggests the typical rather than relative excellence; *Extremum* and *Inflection* feel overly academic; *Spike* suggests transient noise. *Ridge* would revive algorithmic lock-in. |
| **Eliminate: Limits and stalled growth** | *Ceiling, Cap, Plateau, Saturation, Maturity, Limit, Threshold* | Suggest constraints, stopping points, or triggers—unappealing framing for a platform exploring team potential and performance. |
| **Eliminate: Tone or cumbersome length** | *Crown, Climax, Culmination* | *Crown* leans toward royalty/championships rather than quantitative measurement; *Climax* has distracting everyday connotations; *Culmination* is cumbersome to say and display. |

## 5. The Locked Final 3 Shortlist

The search has narrowed to three distinct, high-conviction finalists, each expressing a different dimension of the platform:

```

│                        THE FINAL 3 SHORTLIST                           │
├────────────────────┬────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────┤
│    STRATUM CFB     │     CREST CFB      │         ECHELON CFB          │
│                    │                    │                              │
│  "Depth, Strata,   │  "Peak Trajectory  │  "Upper-Echelon Separation,  │
│   Rock Substance"  │   & Collegiate"    │   Dignity & Prestige"        │
└────────────────────┴────────────────────┴──────────────────────────────┘
```

### 1. Stratum CFB (Top Analytical & Physical Contender)
- **Core Meaning:** Geological layers, structural depth, and competitive stratification. It serves as a natural metaphor for levels of competition: evaluating a team's performance relative to the stratum in which they compete.
- **Creative Character:** Inherits the dense, earthy, physical substance that made *Slate* appealing, paired with genuine analytical credibility.
- **Wordmark & Visuals:** Symmetrical 7-letter balance (`STRATUM CFB`). Lends itself to layered visual motifs, horizontal tier lines, and depth-based data visualization.
- **Considerations:** Requires care with singular/plural usage (*stratum* vs. *strata*), but presents zero direct sports analytics collisions.

### 2. Crest CFB (Top Spoken, Visual & Collegiate Contender)
- **Core Meaning:** Dual resonance:
  1. **Trajectory & Peaking:** A team cresting at the right moment (November/postseason peak performance).
  2. **Collegiate Heritage:** The heraldic, collegiate tradition of a university crest or shield.
- **Creative Character:** The most immediate and energetic brand experience. Compact, punchy, and instantly memorable.
- **Wordmark & Visuals:** Ultra-compact 5-letter punch (`CREST CFB` = 9 characters). Ideal for small mobile header spaces, favicon iconography, and clean badge emblems.
- **Considerations:** Less descriptive of statistical methodology, but provides the cleanest consumer-facing visual canvas.

### 3. Echelon CFB (Top Institutional & Tier-Separation Contender)
- **Core Meaning:** The separation of elite competition—teams operating in the "upper echelon" of college football; tactical flight formations moving forward together.
- **Creative Character:** Authoritative, dignified, and prestigious. Serves as the natural, unencumbered successor to the tone originally sought in *Caliber*.
- **Wordmark & Visuals:** 7 letters (`ECHELON CFB`). Visually refined, elegant in typography, and well-suited for high-end editorial analysis.
- **Considerations:** Slightly more formal and status-oriented; adjacent presence in connected fitness (`Echelon Fit`), but clean in sports forecasting.

## 6. The Active Challenger: Vertex CFB

**Vertex** was evaluated as the sole candidate from the geometric/mathematical exploration capable of challenging the finalists:
- **Strengths:** Sharp phonetics, instantly recognizable, visually compelling. It evokes intersecting lines, coordinate plotting, and data nodes.
- **Assessment:** While technically sophisticated, *Vertex* feels closer to an enterprise software tool, developer library, or 3D graphics engine than a warm, authoritative college football platform.
- **Verdict:** Retained as a documented challenger, but not elevated to the primary top 3 unless the owner explicitly favors geometric branding over athletic substance.

## 9. Implementation Roadmap (Deferred)

No application code, database schema, or production domain will be altered until the site owner makes the final selection. The planned implementation sequence is:

1. **Brand Parameterization:** Centralize brand naming in `web/src/lib/constants.ts`:
   ```typescript
   export const APP_NAME = "CK's Picks CFB"; // Future: "Stratum CFB" | "Crest CFB" | "Echelon CFB"
   export const APP_SHORT_NAME = "CK's Picks"; // Future: "Stratum" | "Crest" | "Echelon"
   ```
2. **Component Wiring:** Decouple `web/src/app/layout.tsx`, `AppBar.tsx`, `Header.tsx`, OpenGraph metadata, and footer copyright to read dynamically from `APP_NAME`.
3. **Asset & Domain Cutover:** Upon final selection, secure matching domain/social handles, design vector crest/logo marks, and update application copy in a single coordinated release.
