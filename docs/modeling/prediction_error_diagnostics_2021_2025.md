# Prediction-error diagnostics, 2021 to 2025

Read-only diagnostics of where V5 prediction error sits. Plain observations only: no candidate fixes, and no model, data, threshold or release change. Generated by `scripts/research/run_prediction_error_diagnostics.py`; rerunning it reproduces this file byte for byte. Contract: [prediction-error diagnostics](../plans/2026-11-12/03-prediction-error-diagnostics.md). Protocol: [offseason research protocol](../plans/2026-11-12/02-offseason-research-protocol.md) (development view 2021 to 2025 only; 2020 excluded; no betting-outcome slices).

## Scope and sources

- Corpus: persisted ratings-lab corpus `ratings-lab/v1/runs/fb0baf90841f51d42958665c/corpus.json` in local lab storage (local-output exception).
- Venue and conference: the `fbs_involved_games` dataset (neutral-site flag, venue id, each team's conference in that season, conference-game flag), reached through the same pinned core-eligibility references as the corpus and joined by season and `game_id`. It was read once, read-only, from the Preview data lake and cached in the local lab output.
- Population: 4,546 games, 2021 to 2025, margin and total each. 2021 comes from the bridge's rolling-origin predictions (fit on 2015 to 2019); 2022 to 2025 are the headline seasons.
- Frozen V5 (`frozen_v5_predictions`, 2022 to 2025, 3,659 games) and the v5-common bridge (alpha-10 Ridge on V5's own pregame states, fit on earlier seasons only) give identical predictions for 2022 to 2025 (checked row by row). Frozen V5 carries no interval, so every table below uses the v5-common rows, which add the 90% intervals and the 2021 season.
- Error is `actual - prediction`. Positive bias means outcomes ran above the prediction.
- Cells under 100 games are marked with `*`; their MAE is noisy. Margin and total are always reported separately.
- Slices use only the model's own outputs, game timing, completed-game stage and evidence-coverage flags. No slice uses market lines, against-the-spread results or over/under results.

## Slice definitions

- Rating-gap size: absolute predicted margin, terciles over all 4,546 games. Edges 5.377 and 12.017 points: small gap at or below the first edge, medium up to the second, large above.
- Totals regime: predicted total, terciles over the same games. Edges 55.812 and 56.994 points: low, mid, high. The middle tercile is narrow because predicted totals are tightly concentrated.
- Week buckets: weeks 1 to 4 individually, then 5 to 8, 9 to 12, 13 to 16. Week 0 is not in the corpus (the earliest week is 1).
- Completed-game stage: the corpus `completed_game_stage` value (0 to 4).
- Site class: neutral site is the dataset's neutral flag. A team's home venues are the venue ids where it hosted at least 3 non-neutral games in 2021 to 2025, plus its most frequent hosting venue. A non-neutral game at any other venue is its own class; a game with a missing flag or venue id would be `venue unknown`.
- Conference group: the home team's conference in that season, grouped as Power (ACC, Big 12, Big Ten, Pac-12, SEC), Group of 5 (American Athletic, Conference USA, Mid-American, Mountain West, Sun Belt), FBS independent, or other. Pac-12 is Power throughout although it had two members in 2024 and 2025. Conference game is the dataset's flag.
- Predicted-total band: quintiles of predicted total, interior edges 55.293, 56.050, 56.724, 57.615 points.
- Partial-quarantine exposure: either team played an earlier-week game, in the same season, in which some but not all team-role PPP observations were quarantined for unresolved scoring attribution. Descriptive only; see the limits below.

## What could not be computed

- **Sensitivity of narrow matchups to the partial-quarantine policy.** A real test re-estimates the ratings with the partially quarantined games removed and re-scores the affected matchups, as in the [V5 ratings audit](v5_offense_defense_ratings_audit_2026.md). The corpus holds frozen ratings and bridge features only, so that re-estimation is not possible here and was not approximated. The exposure table above is a descriptive proxy (2,074 of 4,546 games are exposed); it compares error for games whose teams had an earlier partially quarantined game against those that did not, and says nothing about what the error would be under a different policy.
