# Documentation Archive

This archive preserves useful historical evidence without presenting it as
current authority. Current work starts at the [documentation home](index.md).

## Archived collections

- `archive/2026-rating-research/` — earlier power-rating and adjustment
  investigations; useful context, not an approved design.
- `archive/v2-modeling/` — prior workflow, baseline, promotion, and local
  artifact-layout history.
- `archive/2026-completed-plans/` — strategic buildout records.
- `archive/refactoring/` — completed modernization and resolved incident
  evidence.
- `archive/schema-snapshots/` — historical raw, transformed, and generated
  feature documentation.

Existing archive files outside these collections remain retained historical
records. They may contain obsolete paths, dates, or decisions; do not use them
as operational or architectural authority.
