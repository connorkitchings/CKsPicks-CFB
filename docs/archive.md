# Documentation Archive

This archive preserves useful historical evidence without presenting it as current authority. Current work starts at the [documentation home](index.md). V4 is summarized in the [V4 retrospective](modeling/v4_retrospective.md).

## Archived collections

Only the linked collections are built into this site; the rest are repository-only (paths relative to `docs/` unless noted).

- [V5 contracts](archive/v5-contracts/index.md) — completed and superseded methodology, audit, diagnostic and code-readiness records. Use the [current V5 status](modeling/v5_status.md) for live authority.
- `archive/project_org/` and `archive/experiments/` — V4-era feature registry and the V2 experiment index, including the Odds API historical backfill note.
- `archive/process/` — the MLOps experimentation process from the retired V4 stack.
- `archive/modeling/` — retired V4 early-season regime contract (folded into the retrospective).
- `archive/research/` — September 2026 V5 acceptance and diagnostic reports and their evidence files.
- `archive/reports/` — documentation audit, Phase 0 disposition and baseline, post-V5 cleanup inventories and the local artifact archive manifest.
- `archive/ops/` — superseded runbooks (production runbook, weekly pipeline, V5 operator and shadow runbooks, MLflow MCP).
- [Archived implementation contracts](archive/plans/index.md) — 100 Implemented, Merged or Superseded contracts dated before 2026-10-01 (`archive/plans/YYYY-MM/<date>/`), indexed by date, title and status.
- `archive/2026-rating-research/` — earlier power-rating and adjustment investigations; useful context, not an approved design.
- `archive/v2-modeling/` — prior workflow, baseline, promotion and local artifact-layout history.
- `archive/2026-completed-plans/` — strategic buildout records.
- `archive/refactoring/` — completed modernization and resolved incident evidence.
- `archive/schema-snapshots/` — historical raw, transformed and generated feature documentation.
- `archive/data_science_prompt/` and the loose files directly in `archive/` — retained historical records.
- `session_logs/archive/` — session logs before 2026-10-01, in `daily/YYYY-MM-DD/` (see its `README.md`).
- `archive/` at the repository root — legacy decision log and manifest (`decision_log_legacy.md`, `MANIFEST.md`).

Archived files may contain obsolete paths, dates or decisions; do not use them as operational or architectural authority.
