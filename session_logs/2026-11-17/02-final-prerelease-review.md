# Final pre-release site review: Stratum CFB (desktop and phone)

Read-only review of the whole site on Production data (Week 12 live, publication mode `predictions`), served locally on `http://127.0.0.1:3112` from branch `connorkitchings/final-prerelease-v2` (head `2b0b6a22`). The pass covers desktop widths as the primary target, plus a regression pass on the phone fixes from `01-mobile-review-fixes.md`, and the items that log listed as not visually reviewed. No repo file other than this log was changed; nothing was built and `web/.next` was not touched. A finding closes only on the owner's decision.

## Method

- Playwright (`@playwright/test` from `web/node_modules`) driven by throwaway scripts outside the repo. Viewports: 1440x900 light and dark, 1280x800 light, 1024x768 light; phones 390x844 (`isMobile`, `hasTouch`, `deviceScaleFactor` 3) light and dark, and 320x568 (`isMobile`, `hasTouch`, `deviceScaleFactor` 2) light. Theme forced through `localStorage.theme` plus `colorScheme`.
- Each page: one full-page capture, sliced into viewport-height images and stitched two (desktop) or four (phone) to a contact sheet (magenta gutters are padding). Pages longer than 8 viewports keep the first 7 slices and the last one. Every capture cited below was opened and looked at; where a finding rests on small text, a device-scale (2x) element crop was also opened.
- Automated checks on all 22 page URLs in all 7 viewport and theme combinations (156 runs): `scrollWidth` vs `innerWidth`; visible links, buttons, selects and inputs under 44px on the touch viewports; rendered SVG text size (font size times `getScreenCTM` scale) under 12px; HTML text under 11px; console errors and warnings; failed requests and 4xx/5xx responses; `document.title`, meta description and og/twitter tags.
- Interactions: keyboard Tab sweep on seven desktop pages (computed outline and box-shadow per focused element); theme toggle cycle, client navigation, reload and first-paint class; Games Filters sheet and app Menu sheet on Week 12 and Week 11, and Chart settings on all seven Graphs tabs, at 390 and 320 (Tab x16, body lock, Escape, focus return); chart Download PNG and Copy image on all seven Graphs tabs and on the final and upcoming matchups at 1440 and 390 (download saved and the PNG opened, clipboard read back); Games "All" status with Grid and List layouts, search with a match and with no match, at 1440, 390 and 320; client navigation to Graphs sampled at 60, 200 and 500 ms; table and card overflow probe at 1024 and 1280 on twelve pages; scatter point keyboard focus; axe-core (`@axe-core/playwright`) on 13 pages in light and dark at 1440.
- Capture root: `/private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-connorkitchings-Desktop-Repositories-ckspicks-cfb/dbd07c7a-9df2-4a33-b9b9-3f292ee5c2d3/scratchpad/review/caps/` (scratchpad, not durable). File names: `<page>-<viewport><L|D>-full.png` (full page), `SHEET-<page>-<viewport><L|D>-<n>.png` (contact sheets), `crop-*.png` (2x crops), `dl-*.png` (downloaded exports), `results-*.json` (raw automated check output).

Pages and data used: Games Week 12 (`/`, which defaults to the "Today" status and shows 3 games; `/?status=all` for the full 66) and Week 11 (`/?week=11`, finals); matchups `401856748` (Vanderbilt at Florida, upcoming), `401856857` (Arizona State at UCF, final), `401871078` (Florida International at Kennesaw State, long name); `/teams`, `/teams?view=resume`, `/teams?group=resume` (no `sort`); `/teams/Notre_Dame`, `/teams/Texas`; `/stats?chart=` efficiency, adjusted, downs, finishing-drives, possession, net-success, game-log; `/model`; `/model/method`; `/matchup/999999999`, `/teams/Not_A_Team`, `/nope`; `/ratings`, `/results?week=11`; `/icon`, `/opengraph-image`.
