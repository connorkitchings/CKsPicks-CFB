## Method

- Playwright (`@playwright/test` 1.62.1 from `web/node_modules`) driven by a throwaway script outside the repo. Viewports: 390x844 (`isMobile`, `hasTouch`, `deviceScaleFactor` 3) in dark and light, and 320x568 (`isMobile`, `hasTouch`) in light. Theme forced through `localStorage.theme` plus `colorScheme`.
- Each page: one full-page capture sliced into viewport-height images, stitched four to a contact sheet (magenta gutters are padding, not page content). Pages longer than 8 viewports (Games, Ratings lists) keep the first 7 slices and the last one; the middle slices were not captured (the automated checks still cover the whole page).
- Automated checks per page: document `scrollWidth` vs `innerWidth`; visible links, buttons, selects and inputs under 44px (inline prose links reported separately); rendered SVG text size (font size times screen scale) under 10px; HTML text under 11px; sticky elements while scrolled.

Follow-up probes took device-scale crops of charts and re-ran checks that looked like capture artifacts.
