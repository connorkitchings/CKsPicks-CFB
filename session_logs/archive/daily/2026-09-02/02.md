## Context and Decisions
- Spread Market/Model cells are favorite-relative with full team names
  (e.g. `Texas -2.5`), converting `homeTeamSpreadLine` (home-spread
  convention) and inverting `predictedSpread` (home margin) into one shared
  orientation. When market and model favor different teams, the cells show
  different team names — an honest, visible signal.
- Model cell carries a signed model-minus-market parenthetical, e.g.
  `Texas -3.5 (-1.0)` / `52.0 (+0.5)`, computed from the displayed values;
  hidden when either side is missing (PK or null).
- Model Bet = the leaned team + line taken (spread) or Over/Under + line
  (total), accent-colored as the card's actionable element.
- Fail-closed states preserved: missing line → Market `—` + `No lean`; both
  lines missing → the "No market line" note under the table.
- Bet Result shows Win/Loss/Push chips (existing colors) and `—` pregame.
- Meta row (kickoff, regime chip, ★) and box-score block unchanged, including
  lean-team bolding.
- No team-abbreviation map added — CFBD names are already short-form; full
  names used per user decision.
